About Little Lemon

Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant, focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist.

Our chefs draw inspiration from Italian, Greek, and Turkish culture and have a menu of 12–15 items that rotate seasonally.

The restaurant has a rustic and relaxed atmosphere with moderate prices, making it a popular place for a meal any time of the day.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Little Lemon has been serving the community with delicious Mediterranean cuisine for over a decade.

We believe in creating memorable dining experiences through exceptional food, warm hospitality, and a welcoming atmosphere.
